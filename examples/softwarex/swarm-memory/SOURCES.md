# Source note

`data/germination.csv` is a synthetic, deterministic fixture created for the SwarmX SoftwareX
example. It resembles a small replicated germination comparison solely to make the calculations
easy to inspect. It is not observational or experimental evidence about seed priming.

The defensible result is therefore limited to the fixture: the three control rows have a mean
germination rate of 79%, the three primed rows have a mean rate of 87%, and the difference is
8 percentage points.
